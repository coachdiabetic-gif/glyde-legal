1. What Glyde is, and isn't

Glyde helps you understand how food may affect your blood sugar. It estimates carbohydrates and the likely spike from meal photos, descriptions and barcodes, and offers food tips and a chat coach.

Glyde is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Its results are AI estimates and can be wrong. Glyde:

  • does not measure your blood sugar
  • does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition
  • never gives insulin or medication doses, and never tells you to start, stop or change a medication

Always follow your doctor's or diabetes care team's advice. Never use Glyde to decide an insulin or medication dose. If you feel unwell, faint or confused, or think your blood sugar is dangerously high or low, get urgent medical help.
