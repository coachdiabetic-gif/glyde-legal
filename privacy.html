Glyde Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Glyde ("Glyde", "we", "us") is an app that helps people understand how food affects their blood sugar. This policy explains what information Glyde uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • Your meal history, photos, coach chats and settings are stored on your phone, not on our servers.
  • To analyse a meal or answer a question, Glyde sends that photo, text or recording to AI services to process it. It isn't kept afterwards.
  • Your account stores your sign-in and the answers you gave when setting up your plan.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

Glyde gives estimates. It is not a medical device, doesn't diagnose, and never gives insulin or medication advice.

1. Information stored on your phone

These stay on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • meals you log: the photo (a small copy and a thumbnail), the estimated carbs, fibre, sugar and spike risk, the time, and any corrections you made
  • your profile settings: name, carb target, diet, cuisines, glucose units, meal reminder times, avatar
  • your chats with the Glyde coach
  • saved tips, favourites, achievements and streaks
  • your "Was this accurate?" answers
  • your health answers from setup: your diabetes type, when you were diagnosed, birth year, height, weight, goal weight and your goal. These never leave your phone.

They're removed if you use Profile → Data & privacy → Clear history, delete your account, or uninstall the app. You can export your meal history as a CSV file from the same screen.

2. Information stored with your account

When you create an account, we store the following on our servers (hosted by Supabase):

  • Sign-in details from Apple or Google: an account ID and, if the provider shares it, your email address and name. If you use "Sign in with Apple" with "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Your plan settings: your name, who the app is for, how you heard about Glyde, diet, meals per day, challenges, how you track food, units, carb target, meal times, whether you allowed notifications, and the date you gave your consent during setup.

Your health answers (such as your diabetes type and weight) are not stored on our servers; they stay on your phone. We use your information only to personalise your plan and the app, and only with your consent, which you give during setup.

3. Information sent to be processed (not stored)

When you use these features, the content is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (Supabase Edge Functions), and from there to an AI provider to process it:

FeatureWhat's sent
Scan or upload a meal photothe photo
Type or say what you ateyour text, or your voice recording
Scan a barcodethe product's nutrition information
Fix a resultyour correction, with the original photo or product information
Glyde coachyour recent messages and a short summary of your recent meals, your carb target and your cuisine and diet preferences
Weekly recaplast week's meal counts, average carbs, spike levels and most common meal names

The AI providers are Groq, and OpenAI as a backup. They process the content to return an answer. We don't store your photos, recordings, messages or meal descriptions on our servers: voice recordings are deleted from your phone right after they're turned into text. The AI providers' own policies apply to how they handle data sent to them.

Barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts, a public food database; only the barcode number is sent. Meal idea photos come from Pexels; only the search term (for example "lentil soup") is sent, never anything about you.

4. Information we keep to run the service

For each AI request, we record which feature was used, which AI provider answered, whether it worked and how long it took, linked to your account ID. We also count how many requests you make each day, to apply fair daily limits. These records never contain your photos, recordings, text or chat. We use them to keep Glyde reliable, affordable and fair.

5. Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status (for example, "active until 1 March"), never your card details.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use your health information for marketing.

7. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you clear it, delete your account or uninstall the app.
  • Account and plan settings: until you delete your account.
  • Usage records and daily counts: up to 12 months, then deleted.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Data & privacy → Delete account. This deletes your account, your plan settings, your daily counts and your subscription record from our servers, makes your usage records anonymous (they no longer link to you), and clears the app's data on your phone. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in your iPhone's settings.
  • Export: Profile → Data & privacy → Export my meals.
  • Access or correct: email us and we'll help.
  • Notifications: turn meal reminders off in the app, or in your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), such as asking what we hold about you or objecting to how we use it. Email us to use them.

9. Children

Glyde is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Account data is protected with access rules so each person can only reach their own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
